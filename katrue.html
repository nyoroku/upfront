Spot Fishing Experience on Lake Naivasha

Spot fishing is a guided fishing experience that focuses on specific areas of Lake Naivasha where fish naturally gather. Instead of randomly fishing across the lake, our captains take you to carefully selected spots known for higher fish activity. These spots are chosen based on water depth changes, underwater vegetation, seasonal movement of fish, and local knowledge built over time. Once at the right location, the boat is positioned or gently drifted to give you the best chance of a catch. At Katrue, spot fishing is designed to be relaxed, interactive, and beginner-friendly. Our team explains why each spot works, helps you with basic techniques, and ensures the experience is enjoyable whether you&rsquo;re fishing for the first time or just trying something new on the lake. You may encounter species such as tilapia and catfish, depending on conditions and season. All activities are conducted responsibly, in line with Lake Naivasha conservation guidelines. Perfect for: &bull; Couples looking for a unique lake activity &bull; Families and small groups &bull; Visitors who want more than a standard boat ride &bull; Anyone curious about fishing without committing to a full angling trip Spot fishing adds purpose and excitement to your boat ride &mdash; calm moments, anticipation, and the simple joy of being present on the water.
